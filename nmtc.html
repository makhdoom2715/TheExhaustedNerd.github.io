NMTC

National Mathematics Talent Contest - Pakistan

What is NMTC

NMTC is one of the contests under the National Science Talent Contest (NSTC), functioning under the STEM Careers Programme, a joint venture of PIEAS and HEC. It prepares students to represent Pakistan at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO). The Home Institution for NMTC training camps is COMSATS University Islamabad (CUI), Lahore.

Eligibility

  • Students of Matric OR F.Sc (Part-I) or O-Levels / A-Level (Part-I)
  • 60% or more aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics in the last exam
  • Age less than 20 years on 30 June 2027
  • NOT eligible: F.Sc Part-II, A-Level Part-II, or university students
  • Registration fee: Rs. 1000/- via KuickPay
